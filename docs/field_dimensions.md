# Competition field dimensions used in this starter

Source: ABU Robocon 2027 Rulebook V1.1 and Appendix V1.1 uploaded in this project.

| Feature | Published dimension |
|---|---:|
| Overall Game Field | 11,000 × 11,000 mm |
| Level 1 | 6,000 × 6,000 mm; 600 mm above Ground |
| Level 2 | 3,000 × 3,000 mm; 300 mm above L1 |
| Ground-to-L1 ramp | 3,500 mm long |
| Stair step | 300 mm length × 1,000 mm width × 150 mm height |
| Transfer Area | 1,000 × 1,000 mm |
| L1 Retry Zone | 700 × 700 mm |
| BR Start/Retry size limit | 700 × 700 mm |
| Standard Building Spot | 500 × 500 mm |
| L1 centre-line Building Spot | 750 × 1,000 mm (Appendix drawing) |
| Central Pillar | 270 mm diameter, 800 mm tall |

The Appendix notes the 30 mm L1 perimeter barrier sits inside the 6,000 mm outline,
so the L1 floor surface is 5,940 × 5,940 mm. The schematic world currently uses
the 6 m outer platform envelope; it does not yet model every barrier or opening.
